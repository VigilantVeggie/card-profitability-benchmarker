# Card Profitability Benchmarker
 
An interactive calculator for card product managers, lifecycle marketers, and executives. Model the full economics of a card portfolio — or just the revenue impact of improving activation rates.
 
## Live tool
 
**[Open the Card Profitability Benchmarker →](https://vigilantveggie.github.io/card-profitability-benchmarker)**
 
## What it does
 
**Quick Mode** — 5 inputs, instant results. Enter your activation rate, cards issued, average spend, and interchange rate. See how much revenue you are leaving on the table, the ROI of an activation program investment, and where your activation rate sits against industry benchmarks by institution type.
 
**Full Profitability Mode** — complete card portfolio economics. All revenue streams (interchange, finance charges, fee revenue), all expense buckets (cost of funds, charge-offs, rewards cost, operating expense, acquisition cost), net income per account, 3-year projection, and benchmark comparison against industry averages.
